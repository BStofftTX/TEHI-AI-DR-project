# TEHI Investor Deck

## Slide 1 - Title
**TEHI - The Eyes Have It**

AI-assisted smartphone-first screening prototype for:
- Diabetic Retinopathy
- Cardiovascular Risk

Tagline:
**Your Eyes. Your Health. Detected.**

---

## Slide 2 - The problem
- Diabetic retinopathy is often detected too late to prevent vision damage
- Cardiovascular disease remains a major cause of preventable death
- Rural and underserved populations lack convenient access to specialist screening
- Traditional workflows can be expensive, slow, location-bound, and hard to scale

---

## Slide 3 - The opportunity
TEHI is designed to turn fundus-image capture into a low-friction screening workflow.

Potential value:
- lower-cost screening pathway
- mobile deployment potential
- scalable technician-assisted workflow
- earlier referral and intervention opportunities
- eventual licensing or SaaS deployment pathways

---

## Slide 4 - Product concept
TEHI enables a user or technician to:
1. capture or upload a fundus image
2. run AI-assisted screening
3. receive outputs for:
   - 5-class diabetic retinopathy screening
   - binary cardiovascular risk screening
4. escalate cases for clinician review

---

## Slide 5 - Why now
- smartphone imaging is increasingly viable
- AI-assisted image interpretation is advancing rapidly
- demand for accessible screening is growing
- healthcare systems need lower-friction triage and early detection tools
- underserved and remote markets remain underpenetrated

---
